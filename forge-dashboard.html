The Science Behind FORGE OS

Evidence-based protocols powering your transformation.

Progressive Overload Protocol

Weeks 1–4: Establish form, hit prescribed rep ranges consistently. Build neural pathways.

Weeks 5–8: Increase load — +5 lbs upper body / +10 lbs lower body per session when top of rep range is hit.

Weeks 9–12: Add 1 working set per main compound lift. Volume accumulation phase.

Weeks 13–16: Intensity peak — shift to 4–6 rep range on compounds with heavier loads.

Body Recomposition Science

Simultaneous fat loss and muscle preservation is achievable in trained individuals when protein intake is sufficiently high and training stimulus is maintained.

Schoenfeld et al. 2018 — demonstrated ~1g/lb protein intake supports muscle gain while in a caloric deficit.

Protein Requirements

185g+ daily protein target based on 1.0–1.1g per pound of body weight. This is the single most important nutritional variable for body recomposition. Protein is kept high on both training and rest days — muscle protein synthesis doesn't stop when you leave the gym.

Meta-analysis consensus: 0.7–1.0g/lb is the effective range; higher end preferred during caloric deficit.

Training Split Rationale

Upper/Lower split with dedicated HIIT and active recovery days. This structure allows each muscle group to be trained 2x/week with adequate recovery, which research shows is superior to once-per-week "bro splits" for hypertrophy and fat loss.

2024 meta-analysis — upper/lower splits outperform bro splits for fat loss outcomes in trained populations.

HIIT & EPOC Effect

Saturday HIIT sessions are strategically placed to maximize Excess Post-Exercise Oxygen Consumption (EPOC). High-intensity intervals create an elevated metabolic rate for 12–24 hours post-session, contributing to additional calorie burn beyond the session itself.

EPOC research — HIIT creates 12–24hr elevated calorie burn post-session compared to steady-state cardio.

Sleep & Recovery

Sleep quality directly impacts hormone profiles critical for recomposition — growth hormone, testosterone, and cortisol regulation. Magnesium glycinate before bed supports sleep quality, while casein protein provides sustained amino acid release overnight.

2020 sleep study — sleep education alone improved body recomposition outcomes in resistance-trained subjects.

Strength Tracking: Epley Formula

Estimated One-Rep Max (e1RM) is calculated using the Epley formula:

e1RM = weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)

This allows tracking strength progression without maxing out, reducing injury risk while still measuring improvements across sessions.
